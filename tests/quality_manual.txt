HelixNova Pharma - Quality Manual QM-001

Section 1 - Deviation Management. Any deviation from an approved procedure must be reported to Quality Assurance within 24 hours of detection. The deviation owner records the event in the electronic quality system and assigns an initial risk category of minor, major or critical. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 2 - CAPA. Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) must be opened for every major or critical deviation. Each CAPA must be closed within 30 calendar days unless an extension is approved by the Head of Quality. Effectiveness checks are performed 90 days after closure. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 3 - Batch Record Retention. Executed batch manufacturing records are retained for 7 years after the expiry date of the batch. Records are stored in the secure document archive in Building C and scanned copies are kept in the validated electronic archive. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 4 - Cleanroom Environment. Grade B cleanrooms are maintained at a temperature of 18 to 22 degrees Celsius and a relative humidity of 30 to 60 percent. Environmental monitoring results outside these limits must be escalated to the production supervisor immediately. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 5 - Change Control. All changes to validated equipment, processes or computerized systems require a formal change request. Change requests are approved by the Change Control Board, which is chaired by the Head of Quality Assurance. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 6 - Annual Product Quality Review. An annual product quality review (APQR) is prepared for every commercial product. The APQR must be completed and approved by 31 March of the following year and is presented to senior management. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 7 - Training. All GMP staff complete annual GMP refresher training. New employees must complete induction training before performing any GMP activity without supervision. Training records are maintained in the learning management system. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.

Section 8 - Supplier Qualification. Suppliers of starting materials are qualified through a questionnaire, a quality agreement and an on-site audit. Approved suppliers are re-audited every 3 years, or sooner if significant quality issues occur. This section applies to all HelixNova Pharma manufacturing and packaging sites and to contractors working on their behalf. The document owner reviews this section every two years as part of the periodic review of the quality management system, and any questions about it should be raised with the local Quality Assurance representative.
